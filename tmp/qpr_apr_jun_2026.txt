3. Mobilization and Outreach 
The Mobilization and Outreach Plan is designed to engage rural communities and identify SHGs, SHG 
members, CLFs, Lakhpati Didis, potential Lakhpati Didis, and other individuals with entrepreneurial 
potential, motivation, and interest in establishing or expanding enterprises. Through active collaboration 
with village communities and Gram Panchayat representatives, the initiative facilitates direct engagement 
with rural households to identify individuals possessing skills, traditional knowledge, innovative ideas, or 
livelihood activities that can be transformed into sustainable enterprises. 
The outreach approach emphasizes early identification and nurturing of aspiring entrepreneurs through 
awareness campaigns, community meetings, field interactions, and continuous handholding. These 
engagements help community members recognize business oppor tunities, assess their entrepreneurial 
capabilities, and understand the support available under the incubation ecosystem. Additionally, the 
programme creates awareness about government schemes, financial assistance, capacity -building 
initiatives, and other entrepreneurship support services. Outreach teams also assist potential applicants in 
understanding eligibility requirements, completing documentation, and submitting Calls for Applications 
(CFAs), thereby improving access to entrepreneurship opportunities and promoting inclusive livelihood 
development. 
3.1 Call for Applications 
The Call for Applications (CFA) serves as a key mechanism for identifying and 
mobilizing aspiring entrepreneurs from rural communities. It enables 
engagement with SHG members, CLFs, Livelihood Collectives, Growth 
Centres, Potential Lakhpati Didis, and existing Lakhpati Didis to understand 
their entrepreneurial interests, enterprise ideas, and business aspirations. 
Through the CFA process, outreach teams interact directly with community 
members, gather relevant information on their  backgrounds and livelihood 
activities, and assess their potential for enterprise development and growth. 
The process also helps identify motivated individuals who demonstrate the 
capacity and commitment to establish or scale their businesses.  
During Q1 FY 2026 -27 (April–June 2026), the MUY team successfully filled 
18,994 Calls for Applications (CFAs), reflecting extensive grassroots outreach, 
effective entrepreneur identification, and strong engagement with rural communities across Uttarakhand.

3.4 Entrepreneurship Awareness Program/ Entrepreneurship Development Program 
The Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP)/ Entrepreneurship Awareness Programme (EAP) 
under Mukhyamantri Udyamshala Yojana (MUY) were conducted in convergence with various 
departments and institutions to promote entrepreneurship, self-employment, and livelihood opportunities 
among SHG members, youth, farmers, artisans, and aspiring entrepreneurs. The sessions covered MUY, 
MSY, MSME/Udyam Registration, FSSAI, GST, financial linkages, business planning, marketing, 
branding, packaging, digital promotion , legal compliances, and government schemes. The programmes 
strengthened entrepreneurial skills, facilitated enterprise development, market linkages, and capacity 
building, while encouraging sustainable livelihood generation. 
The Entrepreneurship Orientation Programme aims to: 
• Promote entrepreneurship as a viable self-employment opportunity. 
• Guide and support entrepreneurs at different stages of enterprise development. 
• Encourage utilization of local resources, skills, and traditional knowledge. 
• Enhance income generation and livelihood opportunities. 
• Support enterprise formalization through registrations and compliances. 
• Foster confidence, self-reliance, and financial independence. 
 
Through these efforts, the EDP/EAP plays a vital role in nurturing rural entrepreneurs and strengthening 
the overall entrepreneurial ecosystem across Uttarakhand.

Development Programmes (EDPs), bootcamps, and extensive collaboration

onboarding and incubation support.

entrepreneurship. Nainital (419), Dehradun (285), and Pauri Garhwal (266) 
recorded the highest overall onboarding, while Dehradun (199), Chamoli (149), 
and Udham Singh Nagar (144) led in SHG/CBO mobilization. The achievement 
demonstrates effective outreach, entrepreneur identification, and capacity -
building efforts, contributing significantly to livelihood enhancement, women’s 
empowerment, and sustainable self -employment promotion across 
Uttarakhand.

demonstrated strong conversion performance, with approximately 31% of valid c ontacts expressing 
entrepreneurial interest, while also underscoring the importance of data cleansing, follow-up mechanisms, 
and multi-channel engagement strategies to improve outreach efficiency and beneficiary conversion under
